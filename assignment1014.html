01 Discover

Jingzaijiao Tile-paved Salt Fields

Located in Beimen, Tainan, these iconic salt fields feature geometric shallow pools filled with neatly stacked pyramids of harvested salt. As the sun sets on the horizon, glowing golden light fills the sky and reflects off the calm waters, creating a peaceful, picture-perfect coastal landscape.

02 Historic Streets

Shennong Street

Capturing the charming atmosphere of one of Tainan's best-preserved historic alleys, Shennong Street features traditional two-story wooden shophouses adorned with red lanterns and vintage accents. Visitors casually stroll along the narrow cobblestone path, soaking in the blend of historic architecture, local culture, and modern boutique energy.
